Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Nesty AI ("Nesty", "we") is an AI chat assistant made by Red element. This page explains what information Nesty uses and why. By using Nesty you agree to this policy.

What we collect

  • Account details: your full name, age, email address and password when you create an account, or your name and email from Google if you choose "Continue with Google". Your password is stored by our account provider in protected (hashed) form. You also verify your email with a code we send you.
  • Your chats: the messages you send, Nesty's answers, and the text of any files you attach (PDF, Word or text files), so you can come back and continue your chats later. Files are read on your own device, and only their text is sent. Pictures you attach are sent to an AI provider to answer you or to edit them, and are not saved in your chat history. When Nesty searches the web, the links to the websites it used are saved with the answer. If you tap 👍 or 👎 on an answer, that choice is saved with the chat, and the feedback you send (your reason, plus the question and answer it is about) is sent to us inside Nesty, not by email, so we can improve it. It is linked to your account ID, not shown to other users, and you can ask us to delete it.
  • Custom instructions and memory: the instructions you type in Settings and the facts Nesty saves about you (automatically, or when you ask it to remember something). They are saved with your account, sent to the AI with your messages so answers fit you, and you can edit or delete them in Settings at any time. When you refer to earlier conversations (for example "as we discussed last time"), Nesty also sends short excerpts of your own recent saved chats to the AI with that message, so it can continue where you stopped.
  • Profile picture: the picture from your Google account, or one you upload yourself in Settings, if you choose to have one.
  • Subscription details: your plan, its start and expiry dates, and the activation codes you enter. Payments are made directly to a phone number and checked by hand by the administrator; Nesty does not process payments inside the app. Any payment confirmation you send to the administrator by email is used only to check your payment.
  • Usage counts: simple counts of the messages, file attachments and pictures you use in a time period, used to apply the limits of your plan.
  • Location (only if you turn it on in Settings): your phone shares your position with Nesty once, the app turns it into a place name such as "Kakamega, Kenya", and only that place name is kept on your device. It is sent with your messages so Nesty can give local answers (for example weather) when it helps. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.
  • Device preferences: your chosen app language, background colour, voice language, voice and speaking speed are stored on your own device.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may process things like your IP address and device type to keep the service running and secure.

How we use it

  • To sign you in, show your name, and save your chats.
  • To generate answers, pictures and files for you.
  • To apply plan limits and activate subscriptions.
  • To prevent abuse and keep the service fair for everyone.

We do not sell your personal information and Nesty does not show ads.

Who else handles your data

  • Appwrite Cloud stores accounts and saved chats (their Singapore region).
  • Cloudflare hosts the website and the small server that talks to the other services, runs some AI models (Workers AI), and keeps plan and usage records.
  • AI providers: to answer you, your messages are sent to AI services such as Groq, Google (Gemini) and OpenRouter, which may pass them to the companies that run the models. Free plans of some AI services may use the content to improve their products. Please do not share passwords, ID numbers, bank details or other very private information in chats.
  • Google, if you sign in with Google, tells us your name, email and profile picture.
  • Picture maker: when you ask Nesty to create or edit a picture, your description (and the photo, if you are editing one) is sent to Google's image service (Gemini) or to Cloudflare's AI service, and to Groq so the wording can be improved. For some requests, such as a logo or a place, Nesty first looks up what the subject looks like using Google Search. The picture is shown to you and is not kept in your chat history, so download it if you want to keep it.
  • Web search: when Nesty decides a question needs current information, your question may be sent to Google Search through Google's Gemini service, or to Tavily, a search service, to find up-to-date answers. On the Ultra plan, Deep search sends several search queries made from your question to these services to write a cited report.
  • Football data: when you ask about football scores, tables or fixtures, Nesty fetches public data from ESPN and football-data.org. Your question itself is not sent to them, only the league or club names needed to look the data up.
  • Place lookup: if you press "Use my location" and allow it, your phone sends your coordinates to Nesty's server, which asks BigDataCloud or OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) to turn them into a place name such as "Kibera, Nairobi, Kenya". The coordinates are not saved; only the place name is kept on your device and sent with your messages to Nesty and its AI providers. If your phone cannot find its position, Nesty may use the rough area of your internet connection (city and country) after you press the button. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Code libraries: to read PDF and Word files and to make Excel files, your phone downloads open-source code libraries from jsDelivr, a public code delivery network, which can see your IP address.

Voice, files and notifications

  • Voice: the microphone and voice mode use your browser's built-in speech recognition. In some browsers, such as Chrome, your audio is sent to the browser company (for example Google) to turn it into text. Nesty itself does not store your voice recordings. Spoken answers use the voices on your device, unless you choose a "Nesty natural" voice in Settings: then the text of Nesty's answer is sent in short pieces to Google's speech service (Gemini) to be turned into audio.
  • Word, PDF and Excel files: these are made on your own device from Nesty's answer. They are not uploaded or stored by us.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, Nesty shows a short notification on your phone when an answer arrives while the app is in the background. You can turn this off in your phone or browser settings at any time.

Your choices

  • You can rename, export or delete any chat from the menu, delete all your chats, change your name, photo or password, and close your account in Settings.
  • Closing your account in Settings deletes your chats and stops you from signing in. To have every trace removed, contact us below.
  • You can ask what information we hold about you.

How long we keep data

We keep your account and chats until you delete them or ask us to delete your account.

Security

We use trusted services and keep secret keys on the server, not in the app. No online service is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. AI answers can be wrong, so please check important facts.

Children

Nesty is not meant for children under 13. If you believe a child has made an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Using Nesty after a change means you accept the new version.
